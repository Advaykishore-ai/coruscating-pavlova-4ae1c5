Ritual

The Ritual

How to wear it

A few small habits make a fragrance last longer and sit closer to you.

  1. 01

    Hold it at a distance

    Spray from about 15 cm away so the mist settles evenly on the skin.

  2. 02

    Choose the pulse points

    Wrists, the base of the neck and behind the ears. Warm skin carries the scent.

  3. 03

    Let it settle

    Don't rub your wrists together. Let the top notes open on their own.

Story

Our Story

A scent is the fastest way back to a moment.

Advy began with a fourteen-year-old student and his father, and a shared fascination with scent. Between school days and late evenings of blending, testing and starting over, they built it together, one idea at a time. And behind them both stood one person: a mother to one and a wife to the other, whose patience and belief carried them through every late night.

That partnership is in every bottle: a young, curious nose and a patient, steady hand. Each fragrance is composed to be worn close, to linger softly, and to become part of your story.
